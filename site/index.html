How hosting works

  1. Get a kit. Checkout delivers a download instantly — printable signs, badges, a QR manager, and your organizer guide.
  2. Print and place. Put the six stop signs around your site: a yard, a park loop, a library grounds, an office floor.
  3. Share the start code. Players open the quest on their own phones — no app install — and play by GPS or by the code on each sign.
  4. Pick the mood. Every stop carries four scare levels, Cozy to Overdrive, so the same course plays gentle for little kids and feral for the office bracket.

Full detail lives in the host handbook and the Organizer Toolkit (setup checklist, print specs, QR sign generator, day-of troubleshooting).
